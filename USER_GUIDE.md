## 1. What is Nila? (In Simple Words)

Most task apps stress you out with constant clock alarms while you're busy somewhere else.

**Nila works differently:**
* **It connects your intentions to physical spaces (called *Sanctuaries*).**
* When you step into your **Home**, your evening wind-down rituals and home tasks appear automatically.
* When you are at the **Office**, home chores stay quiet so you can focus.
* When you arrive at the **Market**, your grocery list pops up right when you walk in the door.

> **The Nila Promise:** You only see what matters *where your feet are standing right now*.

## 2. 5-Minute Quickstart

Follow these 4 simple steps to get started:

### Step 1: Log In
1. Open Nila in your web browser.
2. Sign in with your email and password (or create a new account via **Sign up**).

### Step 2: Create Your First Sanctuary
1. Click **My Places** in the sidebar (or bottom menu on mobile).
2. Click **Create Sanctuary** (or **+**).
3. Give it a name (e.g. `Home` or `Office`), pick an emoji (🏡), choose a favorite color, and enter the address or drop a pin on the map.
4. Click **Create Sanctuary**.

### Step 3: Add Your First Intention
1. Go to **Tasks & Rituals** and click **Add New Intention**.
2. Type an intention (e.g. `Water the indoor plants` or `Drink a tall glass of water`).
3. Select your newly created place (e.g. `Home`).
4. Under Trigger, select **When I arrive**.
5. Click **Save Intention**.

### Step 4: Test the Magic!
Look at the bottom-right corner of your screen:
1. Open the floating **Location Simulator**.
2. Toggle **Enable Simulator** ON.
3. Select **Home** from the dropdown.
4. 🎶 *Listen to the gentle arrival chime and watch your Home intention appear automatically in your Active Flow!*

## 3. Understanding "Sanctuaries" (Your Sacred Spaces)

A **Sanctuary** is any physical location where life happens for you. 

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  🏡 Home          🏢 Office        ☕ Favorite Café         │
│  - Rest & unwinding - Focus & standups - Journaling & ideas │
│  - Plant watering  - Deep work goals   - Coffee ritual      │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### What You Can Do Inside Each Sanctuary:

Click on any Sanctuary from **My Places** to open its detail page:

| Feature | How It Helps You | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Contextual Intentions** | Keeps reminders exclusively tied to this space. | *"Take shoes off and stretch"* on arriving Home. |
| **Sacred Notes** | Keep critical information accessible at this exact spot. | Wi-Fi password, door gate code, parking spot number. |
| **Reusable Checklists** | Recurring checklists you can reset anytime with one click. | *"Packing Gym Bag"*, *"Leaving House Checklist"*. |

## 4. Creating & Managing "Intentions" (Tasks & Rituals)

In Nila, we call them **Intentions** because we believe productivity should feel mindful and gentle, not stressful.

### When Adding an Intention:

1. **Title**: What you want to do (e.g., *"Review design presentation"*).
2. **Sanctuary**: Which place this belongs to (or leave empty for a general intention).
3. **Trigger Type**:
   - **`When I arrive` (ENTER)**: Triggers an alert when you physically arrive at the location.
   - **`When I leave` (EXIT)**: Triggers an alert as you leave (e.g. *"Remember your umbrella"*).
   - **`Any time`**: Appears on your list for that place without ringing an alert.
4. **Time Window (Optional)**:
   - Example: Only alert me if I arrive between `08:30 AM` and `10:00 AM`. Outside of that window, Nila stays peacefully quiet.
5. **Priority**:
   - `✦ High` (Rose): Important items that need your focus.
   - `✦ Medium` (Peach): Regular daily intentions.
   - `✦ Low` (Calm Stone): Gentle wishes when you have extra space.

## 5. Navigating Your Daily Rhythm (Home Dashboard)

Your Home screen is your sanctuary hub:

### Top Navigation Bar:
- **Live Clock (e.g., 🕒 5:30 PM)**: Real-time clock ticking smoothly.
- **Dynamic Greeting**: Automatically changes based on time of day:

### Top Right Sanctuary Badge:
- Shows **"You're currently at [Place Emoji + Name]"** with a live status badge so you always know your active space.

### The 3 Dashboard Tabs:
To keep your mind clear and prevent visual overload, Nila shows **one tab at a time**:

1. **🌸 Active Flow**:
   - Your current place context.
   - All intentions you need to focus on *right now* at this location.
2. **✨ Upcoming**:
   - A birds-eye view of all pending intentions across your life, organized in a clean card grid.
3. **🏡 Sanctuaries**:
   - All your saved sacred spaces with intention counters.

## 6. How Arrival & Departure Alerts Work

When you arrive at or leave a Sanctuary, Nila greets you with:

1. **A Soothing 20-Second Ambient Chime**:
   - No harsh phone buzzers or jarring alarm rings. Nila plays a harmonic, peaceful tone synthesized in your browser.
2. **Top Alert Banner**:
   - A clean notification banner slides down from the top of your screen showing the intention.

### Action Buttons on the Alert Banner:
* **Stop**: Silences the chime and marks the reminder acknowledged for this visit.
* **Snooze**: Gives you breathing room and reminds you again in 5 minutes.
* **Hide (✕)**: Dismisses the banner now; checks again in 5 minutes only if you are still at the location.
* **Reschedule**: Opens an instant pop-up sheet to pick a different day or time.

## 7. Journey & Transit (Mindful Travel)

The time spent commuting between places isn't lost time—it is a space to breathe, reflect, and reset.

## 8. Using the Location Simulator (Demo Mode)

Want to see how Nila works without physically walking outside? Use the **Location Simulator**!

1. Look at the bottom-right corner of the screen for the **Location Simulator** widget.

## 10. Client Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: Is my location uploaded to a central server?
**No. Absolutely not.** Nila is engineered with **Zero-Knowledge Privacy**. Your GPS coordinates are processed exclusively in your device's browser memory. No server ever tracks, logs, or stores your location.

### Q: Does Nila work if I lose internet connection?
**Yes!** Nila uses client-side local database storage (IndexedDB). Your sanctuaries, intentions, checklists, and notes remain accessible and functional even when offline.

### Q: Can I use Nila on my phone or tablet?
**Yes!** Nila is fully responsive. On mobile devices, it features an ergonomic bottom navigation bar and touch-friendly controls. You can also add it to your home screen via your browser's "Add to Home Screen" option.

### Q: How do I change my AI API key or provider?
Go to **Settings → Nila AI**. You can enter your Google Gemini or OpenAI API key directly there, or switch providers anytime.

### Q: What if I don't want audio alerts?
You can adjust or silence audio notifications anytime directly from **Settings → Sound & Notifications**.
