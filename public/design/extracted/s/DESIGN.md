## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an affectionate, intimate, and emotionally grounding digital haven. Built for an empathetic companion app, it prioritizes safe, tender, and human-first interactions over clinical utility. The experience feels like unwrapping a warm letter or holding a plush keepsake close to heart.

### Design Direction
- **Tactile Softness & Tonal Warmth:** Soft pillowy surfaces, creamy base tones, and petal-tinted containers convey immediate psychological safety.
- **Gentle Dimensionality:** Subtle, colored ambient shadows and soft edge highlights replace harsh geometric divides.
- **Emotional Tone:** Empathetic, supportive, reassuring, and playful without lapsing into juvenile aesthetics.

## Colors

The palette relies on gentle rose transitions, velvet plum contrasts, and nourishing cream backgrounds to evoke gentleness and closeness.

- **Primary (`#D9658B` - Rose Petal):** The core active accent. Used for high-intent actions, active indicators, and expressive highlights.
- **Secondary (`#F3C5D3` - Soft Blush):** A gentle, comforting fill used for pill tabs, soft borders, interactive card backdrops, and active mood indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#5A2740` - Warm Plum / Deep Berry):** The primary anchoring shade for expressive titles, high-emphasis copy, and micro-delight moments. Provides deep emotional contrast without the coldness of pure black.
- **Neutral (`#FFF8F6` - Warm Cream / Alabaster Silk):** The ambient canvas. Replaces standard sterile whites with a nourishing, warm undertone.

### Semantic Tones & Accents
- **Text Primary:** `#44182E` (Deepest Berry plum for optimal legibility)
- **Text Muted:** `#8D5A72` (Dusty mauve for secondary guidance and timestamps)
- **Surface Elevation 1:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure warm pearl for elevated interactive cards)
- **Surface Elevation 2:** `#FCEEEF` (Tender petal blush for nested modules and message bubbles)

## Typography

The typography couples the sculptural, editorial charm of **Epilogue** for display headers with the human, open geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for interfaces and body copy.

- **Epilogue (Headlines):** Gives emotional presence and warm conviction to titles, companion prompts, greeting banners, and conversational signposts.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body & UI):** Ensures exceptional readability in prolonged dialogues, journal logs, and reflection exercises with balanced counterforms and approachable terminals.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model uses a responsive fluid container system configured around a gentle rhythm of generous padding and cushioned margins.

### Rhythm & Grid Structure
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stream with `1.25rem` screen padding. Vertical spacing prioritizes cohesive flow so thoughts and check-ins feel unified.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 6-column fluid structure with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` screen margins. Supports side-by-side reflection journals and mood check-in modules.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** Fixed-center comfort canvas capped at `1120px` width. Employs asymmetric 8-column layout (e.g., persistent companion avatar/presence dock alongside communicative card streams).

All element spacings scale along an 8-point rhythmic base (with a 4-point half-step for snug chips and icon alignments).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons clinical drop shadows in favor of soft, berry-tinted ambient glows and layered porcelain planes.

### Depth Archetypes
- **Base Canvas:** Flat `#FFF8F6` provides a relaxed foundational warmth.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Pill Items):** Surface tinted in pure warm white `#FFFFFF` layered over the base canvas, lifted by an ambient tinted shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(90, 39, 64, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(90, 39, 64, 0.03);`
- **Level 2 (Floating Action Trays & Popovers):** Elevated emotional cues and floating input bars:
  `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -6px rgba(90, 39, 64, 0.10), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(90, 39, 64, 0.04);`
- **Inner Depth (Recessed Textareas & Mood Wells):** Gentle pressed inset for quiet spaces:
  `box-shadow: inset 0 2px 4px rgba(90, 39, 64, 0.04);`

## Shapes

The design system embraces high curvature (`rounded-2xl`, `rounded-3xl`, and continuous pill forms). Sharp corners are entirely avoided to sustain a non-threatening, tender sanctuary feel.

- **Small Interactive Units (Chips, Buttons, Badges):** Full pill (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Cards & Prompts:** `24px` to `32px` (`rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl`) ensuring cards feel pillow-soft.
- **Companion Avatar & Expression Enclosures:** Fluid, rounded organic geometries and soft squircle containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in Rose Petal (`#D9658B`) with crisp white or deep berry text (`#FFFFFF`). Fully pill-shaped with `0.75rem 1.75rem` padding. Subtle scale transition (`scale: 0.98`) on active tap.
- **Secondary / Tender:** Pale blush fill (`#FCEEEF`) with Plum text (`#5A2740`). No harsh outer border; uses a warm translucent rim (`rgba(217, 101, 139, 0.15)`).
- **Ghost / Empathic:** Transparent background, muted berry text (`#8D5A72`), shifting to soft blush on hover.

### Chips & Emotion Tags
- Pill-shaped tags with `0.5rem 1rem` padding.
- **Unselected:** Subtle cream-rose background (`#F9ECEF`) with muted plum text.
- **Selected:** Soft rose petal surface (`#F3C5D3`) bordered by a `1.5px` stroke of `#D9658B`, accompanied by deep berry text.

### Cards & Dialogue Panels
- Warm white base (`#FFFFFF`) with `28px` corner radiuses and ambient berry shadow.
- Inner padding ranges from `1.5rem` to `2rem`.
- Interactivity triggers a subtle translation (`translate-y: -2px`) paired with a deeper plum-tinted bloom shadow.

### Input Fields & Conversation Box
- Generously rounded (`rounded-2xl` for multi-line inputs, full pill for single-line inputs).
- Background set to `#FFFFFF` with a quiet blush border (`#F3C5D3`).
- **Focus State:** Subtle bloom halo (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(217, 101, 139, 0.20)`) and deep plum caret.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Organic circles with `22px` diameter.
- Custom tick/dot rendered in smooth rose petal tones over warm cream wells, avoiding harsh utilitarian checks.

### Companion-Specific Components
- **Heartbeat / Mood Orb:** Floating organic gradient orb (`#D9658B` to `#F3C5D3`) with continuous gentle scale animation representing companion presence.
- **Affection Bubbles:** Conversational speech clouds featuring asymmetric curvature (e.g., `rounded-3xl` with a singular `rounded-sm` anchor corner pointing toward the speaker).
- **Daily Reflection Carousel:** Horizontal snap container with smooth overflow fade and pill pagination beads.
